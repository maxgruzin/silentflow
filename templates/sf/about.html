About Silent Flow

A place for music
to find its people.

Silent Flow is an independent, non-commercial netlabel for ambient, experimental and jazz music. We connect curious listeners with artists following their own sound.

Made to be heard.

From slow-moving textures to unexpected rhythms, our catalogue leaves room for exploration. Every release is available to listen to and download for free.

Our purpose is simple: help artists share the music they make, and help that music reach people who will care about it.

The artists come first.

Artists keep the rights to their work and remain free to publish and distribute it themselves. Silent Flow does not commercially benefit from the music we share.
